The **QU**antum **A**lgorithmic **I**ntelligence **L**aboratory (QUAIL) is a research lab at University College Dublin focused on three main research areas:
1. Quantum Optimisation
2. Machine Learning for Quantum Computing
3. Quantum Computing for Machine Learning

To learn more about our lab, please visit [ucd-quail](https://ucd-quail.github.io/).
